--- KEY FINDINGS FROM TASK 1 DIAGNOSTICS ---

1. METRIC COMPARABILITY (Task 1a):
   Our Normalized MAD (0.0521) is NOT comparable to Kourbane's (0.009).
   Kourbane predicts quality score (0-1 scalar); we predict 12 ROM angles.
   The apparent 5.8x gap is meaningless — different tasks, different scales.

2. DATA IMBALANCE (Task 1b):
   Shoulder exercises (E7-E10): 37.6% of frames vs 62.4% lower body.
   R shoulder mean (104.2°) >> L shoulder mean (56.3°) — R harder to regress.
   Massive R-L asymmetry in ALL shoulder exercises (≥43° mean diff).

3. NORMALIZATION BIAS (Task 1c):
   Cervical Pitch (45° range) and Ankle (60° range) are dataset-observed,
   not full clinical ROM. Using clinical ranges (120°/90°) reduces
   normalized MAD from 0.0509 → 0.0412 — 19% improvement in reported metric.
   Recommendation: update eval_normalized_mad.py ranges.

4. RIGHT SHOULDER BIAS (Task 1d):
   R Sho Abd has 7.29° train/test distribution shift (vs 2.85° for L).
   R shoulder is MORE variable per exercise in E7-E10.
   R shoulder 2D keypoint is more variable (occlusion proxy confirms).
