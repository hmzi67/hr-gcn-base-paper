--- ROOT CAUSE SUMMARY ---
  1. Variability: R Sho Abd std=36.09°  vs L Sho Abd std=39.19°
     L has higher variability
  2. Train/test shift: R Sho Abd 7.29° vs L Sho Abd 2.85°
     R shoulder distribution in test differs more from train — generalisation gap.
  3. Right shoulder joints lack bilateral symmetry in some exercises (see above).
  4. Occlusion: right-side exercises (E8 Shoulder Extension) emphasize right arm,
     left-only exercises are rarer in UI-PRMD → right arm more ambiguous to 2D→3D.

RECOMMENDATIONS:
  - Apply left-right mirror augmentation more aggressively (p_mirror=0.7)
  - Use joint weights: R_sho_abd=3.0x, L_sho_abd=2.0x (Task 2b)
  - Use exercise weights: E8 RSA/RSF = 2.5x (Task 2a)
